Family Dashboard

Family Dashboard is a private, self-hosted dashboard for a single household. It runs on a home computer and is used only by the members of that household. It is not a public service, and new users can't sign up.

With a household member's permission, it reads their Google Calendar events and recent Gmail inbox message headers so the family can see upcoming plans and relevant email in one place. It has read-only access and never sends, edits, or deletes anything in a Google account.

See the privacy policy for details on what data is accessed and how it is handled.
